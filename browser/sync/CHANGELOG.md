## Unreleased

- Records (cookie, bookmark, extension, setting, tab, history) with stable keys, hybrid logical clock stamps and a merge that gives every device the same result in any order. Cookies are off until turned on, a site can be excluded by any of its host names, and cookies that would be no use on another device never move. A clock that was wrong once is put right: no stamp is given or taken more than ten minutes ahead.
